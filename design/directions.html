Each direction below is live, not a picture. The big frame is the home page on a laptop and the small one is the same page on a phone. Try the interaction in each one, then reply in chat with 1, 2 or 3.

Positioning

For South Africans who tow something that matters, US Trailers designs and builds one trailer at a time in Cape Town, engineered around your vehicle, your licence code and your load.

The one feeling

"This is being drawn for me, by people who care about every millimetre and every weld."

1

Drawing Office

The site is the drawing board. Drafting paper, Prussian-blue ink and real dimensions in millimetres. Every trailer starts as a sketch and becomes steel in front of you. It feels exact, calm and personal, the opposite of a dealer catalogue.

Try it: drag the Sketch → Steel slider.

2

Night Shift

The workshop after hours. Gunmetal, zinc and the glow of a welding arc. Huge industrial type, and a trailer you can see straight through: the paint gives way to the galvanised frame, the welds and the wiring. It sells build quality by showing what other builders hide.

Try it: move over the trailer (or drag on a phone). Press and hold to widen the X-ray.

3

Tankwa Hours

The trailer where it belongs: out in the Karoo. A quiet editorial serif, open sky and real light. Switch between day, dusk and night, and at night the trailer's LEDs come on under the stars. It sells the life the trailer makes possible, and feels like a commission rather than a purchase.

Try it: switch Day, Dusk and Night.

My recommendation

Go with 1, Drawing Office

Buyers' biggest fears are build quality and paperwork, and a site built like an engineering drawing answers both before they ask: real dimensions, real parts, licence code and weight shown up front. It's the clearest way to say "made only for you", and no trailer builder anywhere looks like it, while dark 3D sites like 2 are common among award winners. It also carries over to the rest of the site: the sketch becomes the part-by-part 3D build, the configurator becomes "your drawing", and the X-ray and night-light ideas from 2 and 3 can still appear inside it.
