02

Shot list

One afternoon with your phone. Each shot sells something the drawings can't.

Light

Late afternoon, the hour before sunset, or a bright overcast day. Never midday sun.

Phone

Clean the lens. Hold it sideways for photos and the website, upright for Instagram and TikTok. 4K, 30 fps.

Steady

Lean on something or move slowly. Hold every clip for at least 8 seconds.

  1. 01
    The hero

    A finished trailer hitched to its owner's bakkie, front three-quarter view, at standing height. Somewhere real: a farm road, the beach car park, the start of a gravel pass.

    Photo + 10 s clip
  2. 02
    Side on

    Straight side view, whole trailer in frame, phone at hub height. This matches our drawings.

    Photo
  3. 03
    Ramp down

    Film the rear ramp being lowered, then a bike or quad riding up it.

    20 s clip
  4. 04
    Under the trailer

    Lie on the ground and shoot along the chassis: galvanised rails, cross members, suspension.

    Photo + slow pan
  5. 05
    A weld, close

    A finished weld bead, phone 10 cm away, light from the side so the ripples show.

    Photo
  6. 06
    Welding in action

    Someone welding, filmed from behind their shoulder. Sparks, mask, gloves. Safety first: keep the phone back.

    15 s clip
  7. 07
    Hands at work

    Measuring, marking steel, tightening a bolt. Hands and tools, no faces needed.

    3 short clips
  8. 08
    The workshop

    One wide shot of the whole workshop from a corner, lights on, floor swept.

    Photo
  9. 09
    The team

    Everyone who builds, standing in front of a trailer, looking at the camera. Natural, not posed.

    Photo
  10. 10
    On the road

    From a second vehicle, film the trailer being towed past on gravel. Or a phone clamped on the bakkie looking back.

    20 s clip
  11. 11
    Night

    After dark, all trailer lights on, awning light lighting the ground.

    Photo
  12. 12
    Handover

    The moment the owner gets the keys or first hitches up. Ask their permission to use it.

    Photo + clip

Send them to me in our chat, or share a Google Drive folder. Full-size files, not WhatsApp-compressed if you can.

03

Google Business Profile, in 10 minutes

This is how you show up on Google Maps and in "trailer builders Cape Town" searches.

  1. 01
    Open Google Business

    Go to business.google.com and sign in with the Google account you want to own the listing.

  2. 02
    Add your business

    Name: US Trailers. Category: start typing "Trailer manufacturer" and pick it. Add "Trailer repair shop" as a second category later.

  3. 03
    Location

    If clients can visit the workshop, add the address. If not, choose "I deliver goods and services to my customers" and add Cape Town and the Western Cape as your service area.

  4. 04
    Phone and website

    Add your number and this website:

    https://us-trailers.vercel.app

    Change it to ustrailers.co.za once the domain is connected.

  5. 05
    Verify

    Google picks the method, often a short video showing your signage, workshop and tools, or a phone call. Have the workshop tidy and your sign visible.

  6. 06
    Fill it in

    Opening hours, a two-line description ("Custom toy haulers, goosenecks, off-road trailers and upgrades, built to order in Cape Town."), and photos from the shot list, at least 10.

  7. 07
    Ask for reviews

    Send the review link to your last five happy clients. Reviews matter more than anything else on the listing.
